## Not recommended

- Buying links, link exchanges, PBNs, mass directory submissions, automated outreach.
- Invented awards, "Top 10" badges, fake review counts, anonymised-but-invented case studies.
- More city × service pages or a "KI Telefonassistent Vergleich" page without a disclosed method and verified data.
- Adding `AggregateRating` or review schema before real, consented reviews exist.
